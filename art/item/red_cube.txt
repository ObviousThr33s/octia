# octia:red_cube - the sealed cube.

# the same way, and the only thing that tells them apart is the ribbon: over
# the lid, across the seal, and straight down the near edge. That is on
# purpose - a player who has learned to read one has learned to read all
# three, and the colour is then the whole message.
#
# 'r' is sealed red and docs/PALETTE.md reserves it for this cube alone.
# Andesite lit on the lid, mid on the left face, dark on the right, which is
# the same three-tone reading every other andesite thing in this mod gets.
